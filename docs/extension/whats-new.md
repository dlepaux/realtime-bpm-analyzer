# What's new

Live BPM Detector just updated. Here is what changed.

<ExtensionWhatsNew>

## What stays on your computer

Your readings history and a small count of the tempos shown, which only decides
when to ask about a rating, are kept in this browser, on this computer. Nothing
is sent anywhere. You can turn the history off, remove a reading or clear them
all on the extension's page. The [extension's privacy policy](/extension/privacy)
has every detail.

**Free tools on the website:**
[BPM Finder](https://app.realtime-bpm-analyzer.com/) ·
[Tap Tempo](https://app.realtime-bpm-analyzer.com/tap-tempo) ·
[Online Metronome](https://app.realtime-bpm-analyzer.com/metronome) ·
[BPM to ms](https://app.realtime-bpm-analyzer.com/bpm-to-ms)

</ExtensionWhatsNew>
